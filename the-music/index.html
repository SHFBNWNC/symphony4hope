Artistic Director

Dr. Michael Dodds

Professor of Music, UNC School of the Arts  ·  Director of Worship, Music & the Arts, First Presbyterian Church of Winston-Salem

Dr. Dodds composed his previous choral symphony, I Will Exalt You (inspired by Psalm 145), for the 40th anniversary of Crisis Control Ministry in 2013. That work led to the feature-length documentary Blessed Unrest, narrated by revered actor Rosemary Harris. The film screened at the 2023 RiverRun Festival and has won more than a dozen awards on the international film festival circuit.

About the Music

The O Antiphons

In these sonnets, Malcolm Guite responds poetically to each of the seven “O” antiphons — plainchants that since the sixth century have been sung before and after the Magnificat, Mary's song, on the seven evenings leading up to Christmas Eve. Boethius references these anonymous chants as early as c. 515 AD. Each antiphon invokes a Biblical title of Christ and concludes with a plea for Christ to come.

Read backward from the vantage of Christmas Eve, the first letters of the antiphons spell out ERO CRAS — “tomorrow I will be with you.” These antiphons inspired the Advent hymn O Come, O Come, Emmanuel. Taken together, these songs trace an arc from deep need to hope to fulfillment, from darkness to light.
